SchoolSmartEYE AI
Investor showcase · concept & engineering overview

Local-first intelligent-school ecosystem

Practical AI for schools.
Closer to the school.

SchoolSmartEYE AI is being developed around a distributed local AI architecture that aims to coordinate suitable workloads across ordinary school computers, connect approved school knowledge, and support carefully governed safety-oriented research.

Explore the interactive concept demo →View public GitHub repository
Readiness statement: This showcase combines an architectural vision, preserved experimental foundations, and a simulated front-end walkthrough. It is not a claim of a production-ready school platform, validated five-device deployment, live camera/wearable integration, or proven prediction accuracy.
