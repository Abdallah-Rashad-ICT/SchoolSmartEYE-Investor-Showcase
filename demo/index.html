What must be proven next

1 · Secure execution

Authenticated PC1-to-worker execution, enrollment, authorization, and replay protection.

2 · Hardware evidence

Repeatable latency, resource, reliability, and cost benchmarks on ordinary school computers.

3 · One-school pilot

Approved sample workflows, privacy review, staff feedback, and measured acceptance criteria.

Responsible AI note: use synthetic data in demonstrations. No facial recognition, emotion inference, attention scoring, or automated disciplinary decisions are presented as product capabilities. Safety-related outputs must remain advisory and human-reviewed. This concept demo does not establish production readiness, performance, cost savings, or validated incident prediction.
