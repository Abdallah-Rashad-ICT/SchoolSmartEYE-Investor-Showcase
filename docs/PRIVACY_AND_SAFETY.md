# Privacy, Safety, and Responsible AI

SchoolSmartEYE AI is intended for school environments. Child privacy, safeguarding, and human accountability are core design constraints.

## Design principles

- Prefer local processing where practical.
- Use synthetic, non-identifying data for development and investor demonstrations unless a separately approved dataset is available.
- Enforce school scope and least-privilege access.
- Require approved-document controls before school content is used for retrieval.
- Minimize collection and retention, with documented access and deletion processes.
- Require human review for consequential safety, academic, disciplinary, or wellbeing decisions.
- Clearly label experimental, simulated, and validated functionality.

## Vision and safety

Visual signals must not be treated as facts or diagnoses. Research outputs require evaluation for false positives, false negatives, environmental limitations, and bias. A possible safety alert is advisory and must be reviewed by a trained human.

No facial recognition, emotion recognition, attention scoring, classroom ranking, or automated discipline is presented as an acceptable target use.

## Wearables

Wearable-related work is currently blocked pending redesign and formal governance. Any future pathway must be voluntary, opt-in, purpose-limited, and independently reviewed. The system must remain usable when a student or staff member declines a device. No inference of comprehension, emotion, focus, stress, or mental state from biometrics is an acceptable goal.

## Data handling

Never use real student data in an investor demonstration. Never publish secrets, credentials, device identifiers, private endpoints, student records, or raw physiological data in this public repository.

## Scope of these principles

These principles describe intended engineering requirements. They are not a legal opinion, privacy certification, safety certification, or proof that every control has already been implemented.
